Turn Urban Data into Meaningful Spatial Insights

Urban planning involves more than mapping locations. It requires understanding spatial patterns, measuring accessibility, identifying suitable locations, analyzing urban growth, and supporting better planning decisions. This course introduces practical GIS techniques for addressing these challenges through urban spatial analysis and modeling with ArcGIS Pro.

You will measure building coverage ratios and floor area ratios at the parcel level, analyze population density and urban form, and identify patterns of population change, street density, traffic accidents, and urban risks. You will also evaluate locations with spatial criteria and suitability models, work with transportation networks and OpenStreetMap data, and apply location-allocation methods for urban facility placement.

Spatial statistics such as Average Nearest Neighbor, Global and Local Moran’s I, Hot Spot Analysis, directional distribution, and clustering help you investigate urban patterns. The course finishes with urban growth simulation, temporal analysis with the Time Slider, 2D and 3D population density mapping, and street-level sunlight and shadow assessment.

What You Will Learn

Practical skills covered in the course.

  • Calculate building coverage ratios, floor area ratios, and population density at parcel, neighborhood, and district scales.
  • Analyze urban form, population change, street density, traffic accidents, and urban risk zones using spatial analysis tools.
  • Perform distance analysis, zonal statistics, and data reclassification to evaluate urban conditions and service accessibility.
  • Select suitable urban sites using spatial criteria, suitability models, and conformity analysis.
  • Build transportation networks and perform optimal routing, service area, and nearest facility analyses.
  • Apply location-allocation and origin–destination analysis to improve urban service accessibility and population coverage.
  • Use spatial statistics, including Moran’s I, Hot Spot Analysis, clustering, and spatial outlier detection, to identify urban patterns.
  • Analyze urban growth, population distribution, land-use patterns, and parcel subdivision using spatial analysis techniques.
  • Simulate urban growth, land acquisition, and urban incidents over time, and visualize temporal changes using the Time Slider.
  • Create 2D and 3D population density maps, urban animations, and street-level sunlight and shadow analyses.

Requirements

Before you start.

  • Basic knowledge of urban planning or geography is helpful but not mandatory.
  • ArcGIS Pro installed on your computer.
  • No programming experience is required.

What You Will Gain

  • Practical experience with urban spatial analysis using ArcGIS Pro.
  • Skills in site suitability, transportation networks, and accessibility analysis.
  • An understanding of spatial statistics and urban pattern detection.
  • Techniques for analyzing urban growth and visualizing spatial changes over time.
  • A broader toolkit for applying GIS to urban planning and spatial decision-making.

Who This Course Is For

Designed for urban planners, GIS analysts, geography students, urban researchers, and spatial data professionals who want practical skills in urban spatial analysis and modeling using ArcGIS Pro. Suitable if you work with urban density, land-use patterns, service accessibility, network analysis, site selection, spatial statistics, or urban growth simulation.

  • Urban Planning Students
  • GIS Analysts
  • Geographers
  • Urban Researchers
  • Spatial Data Professionals
