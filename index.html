Go Beyond Mapping. Understand Cities Through Spatial Analysis.

Urban planning requires more than visualizing locations on a map. It involves understanding spatial patterns, measuring accessibility, evaluating urban conditions, identifying suitable locations, and analyzing how cities change over time.

This course introduces practical GIS techniques using ArcGIS Pro to help learners investigate urban challenges and develop stronger spatial analysis skills.

What You Will Learn

The course moves through urban form, accessibility, site selection, networks, spatial statistics, growth, and three-dimensional analysis.

  1. Urban Form and Density Analysis

  2. Building Coverage Ratio (BCR) and Floor Area Ratio (FAR)

  3. Population Density and Urban Risk Analysis

  4. Spatial Site Selection and Suitability Modeling

  5. Transportation Networks and Optimal Routing

  6. Urban Service Accessibility Analysis

  7. Location-Allocation and Facility Planning

  8. Spatial Statistics and Hot Spot Analysis

  9. Urban Growth Simulation and Temporal Analysis

  10. 2D and 3D Urban Analysis and Shadow Assessment

Practical Applications

These methods help examine a city with spatial evidence. They support study and decision-making. They do not replace local knowledge or the limits of the data.

  • Urban planning studies

    Compare form, density, land use, and change across parcels, neighborhoods, and districts.

  • Spatial decision-making

    Set criteria, compare locations, and see how a proposal relates to existing conditions.

  • Site selection

    Test whether candidate sites meet spatial conditions such as access and compatibility.

  • Public service planning

    Estimate where facilities could cover more people, and where coverage is thin.

  • Accessibility assessment

    Measure reach along the street network. See the guide to urban accessibility analysis.

  • Urban growth monitoring

    Follow building, block, and population change over time.

  • Spatial pattern detection

    Ask whether density or incidents are clustered. Related: population density analysis in ArcGIS Pro.

Who Is This Course For?

Basic knowledge of urban planning or geography is helpful but not mandatory. Programming experience is not required. ArcGIS Pro needs to be installed.

  • Urban Planning Students
  • GIS Analysts
  • Geographers
  • Urban Researchers
  • Spatial Data Professionals
